How the automation works

A routine that finishes without asking you anything.

Most "automations" on iOS stop at a confirmation dialog. SpotiActions and AutomateMusic expose native App Intents, so the system resolves the whole chain in the background — including from a locked screen.

01 · Trigger

An alarm, a time of day, a CarPlay connection or a Focus change fires a native iOS automation.

02 · App Intent

Shortcuts resolves an intent the app publishes — with real parameters: playlist, station, shuffle mode, target device.

03 · Framework

The intent calls MusicKit or the Spotify Web API directly, inside the app's own background execution window.

04 · Result

Playback starts. No unlock, no tap, no confirmation screen — the chain completes end to end.
