Privacy Policy

Effective 24 August 2026

The short version. DataFastView has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. Everything you configure stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone. We — the developer — receive nothing, and could not identify you if we wanted to.

1. Who we are

DataFastView is developed and published by Gonzalo Moscoso Pacheco (myiosapps.org). Questions about this policy go to support@myiosapps.org.

2. Data we collect

None. The app has no server. There is no sign-in, no profile, no identifier assigned to you, no crash reporting service and no analytics framework of any kind.

3. Data the app stores on your device

All of the following lives in the app's private container and its App Group, so the app and its widgets can read the same values. It is removed when you delete the app.

WhatWhy
Your settings — chosen metrics, colours, phrases, 12/24-hour clockTo show what you configured.
Your launch actions and their iconsTo draw the Actions widget and Control Center buttons.
An optional wallpaper screenshot you load yourselfTo make the Actions widget read as transparent.
The last Wi-Fi and storage readingSo the widget can show something when the app is not running.
The last forecast, and the coordinates and place name it was fetched forSo the weather widget can draw without waking the app.

4. Location

If you allow it, the app reads your location while you are using it, for one purpose: to ask Apple's weather service what the forecast is where you are, and to write a readable place name for the screen and the widget.

  • The coordinates are sent to Apple as part of the weather request, and nowhere else.
  • They are stored on your device only, so the widget can refresh without opening the app.
  • They are never sent to us. We have no server to send them to.
  • Declining is fine — every other part of the app works without it.

5. Local network

The Wi-Fi quality score is built from the round-trip time to your own router, which requires the local network permission iOS asks for. The app sends timing probes to the router address only. It does not scan your network, enumerate devices, read their names, or connect to anything else on it.

6. Network requests the app makes

  • Latency probes to your router and to a public endpoint, to measure the numbers shown on the Now tab.
  • A download-speed test, only when you tap the button that starts it.
  • A weather request to Apple's WeatherKit service, if you allowed location.
  • App icon lookups against Apple's public App Store search endpoint, when you pick an icon for an action.

Apple's handling of WeatherKit requests is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather; see Apple's data source attribution.

7. The password generator

Passwords are generated on your device using the operating system's cryptographic random source. They are never transmitted and never written to disk. Copying one places it on the system clipboard with an expiry so it does not sit there indefinitely; while it is there, it is subject to the normal behaviour of the iOS clipboard, including Universal Clipboard if you have it enabled.

8. Children

DataFastView is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

9. Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the Advertising Identifier, and does not present the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to ask for.

10. Your rights

Rights of access, correction, deletion and portability apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none. Everything the app keeps is on your own device and under your control: delete the app and it is gone.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
