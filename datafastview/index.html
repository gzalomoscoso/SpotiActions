Made to be looked at

Finished widgets. Nothing to build.

No editor, no templates to tweak. Each widget is already designed and shows your real data — calendar, reminders, the weather, steps, unread mail — the moment you add it.

Transparent, by one switch

Touch and hold a widget, tap Edit Widget, turn on Transparent. The card disappears and the text, charts and icons sit straight on your wallpaper.

Day, night, or your choice

Each widget follows your iPhone's light and dark appearance, or stays in the one you pick. Same hierarchy either way.

Nothing leaves your phone

Your calendars, reminders, steps, health readings and mail are read on the device. No account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.

Pricing
Free app.
Pay once per page.

No subscription, no account, no trial that ends. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store, in your currency.

  • Three widgets are free for everyone.
  • Live previews of every widget before you buy, drawn from your own calendar, mail and steps.
  • A page bought once is unlocked on every iPhone signed in with your Apple Account. Restore it on a new phone with one tap.
  • Every widget, bought or free, can be transparent.
Three steps

On your Home Screen in a minute.

  • 1Open the app once. It asks only for what the widgets need — calendar, reminders, location for the weather. Health is asked for only once you own the Health page.
  • 2Add a widget. Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for DataFastView.
  • 3Make it transparent. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, turn on Transparent.
And the rest of the app

More than widgets.

  • Status — free space, link quality, a real speed test (download, upload, latency, jitter) and ping.
  • Dynamic Island — fourteen live metrics, or a phrase of your own, in the colour you pick. Switch it on from Control Center.
  • Control Center — your shortcuts one swipe away.
  • Passwords — strong passwords and passphrases, ready to paste.
  • Yours to set — day or night, two backdrops, English or Spanish whatever your iPhone speaks.
Private by design

Read on the device. Shown on the device.

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.
  • Mail widgets connect straight to your mail server over TLS; passwords stay in the Keychain.
  • Health is read-only — steps, distance and blood pressure — and only ever displayed.
  • Weather comes from Apple Weather, with attribution on the widgets.

Make your iPhone yours.

Free to download. Three widgets free. The rest, one purchase per page — and yours forever.
